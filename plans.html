Good to know

  • Plans are bought and managed in Google Play. The app never sees your payment details.
  • If a plan ends, the app keeps every recording for 30 days before the Free limit applies again. Nothing is deleted on the day a plan stops.
  • Recordings the app keeps are also copied to the phone's Download folder, on every plan. Those copies are yours and stay until you delete them.
  • Prices shown in US dollars. Google Play shows the price for your country and handles refunds. See the terms.
