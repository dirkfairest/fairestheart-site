Get started

The strap, the app, the fit, and the first day.

About ten minutes to set up. Then the phone does the rest.

The strap

A Polar H10 chest strap, any generation that streams ECG. Many people already own one, and a second-hand one works. Wrist devices and other straps do not.

The app

Install Fairest Heart on an Android phone, 8.0 or newer. Open it and follow the first-run steps; they ask for Bluetooth and notification access. The iPhone version is coming.

Get it on Google Play

The fit

Wet the two electrode areas on the band, with water or a little gel. Wear it snug, just under the chest muscles, with the logo centred. Within a minute the strap signal in the app should read Good. If it says Poor, wet the electrodes again and tighten the band one notch.

The first recording

Connect the strap once. From then on the app starts recording by itself whenever the strap connects, and a notification shows that it is running. Leave the phone alone: the phone can lock. The first-run steps include one battery-saving setting for your phone brand; do it, or Android may stop the recording in the night.

The morning

Open the Insights page for the night and the day. The Recordings list holds every recording. Tap Share to send one as a PDF, or as EDF+ for software that reads it. Every recording is also copied to the Download/FairestHeart folder.

If something feels off

Tap the Mark bar in the app. It marks that moment in the recording. You can add what you felt, what you were doing, or a note, later. Then show the file or the PDF to your clinician.

Stuck? See Help, or write to help@fairestheart.com.

Fairest Heart is not a medical device and not a replacement for a recorder from your clinic. It records, displays and exports the data your sensor provides. It does not analyse your heart rhythm and makes no statement about it. If you are worried about your heart, see a clinician.
