Changelog

What changed, release by release.

The version is on the menu's bottom line in the app. Google Play updates in the background. Earlier numbers are the test builds the app went through before anyone else saw it.

0.100.8October 2026First release

First release on Google Play. Records, shows and shares your Polar H10 ECG, heart rate, R-R intervals and HRV. Everything stays on your phone.

  • The Help page links to the privacy policy, this website and the support address.
  • The live R-R, HRV and pNN50 tiles are on the ECG tab from the start.
  • PDF with a hospital-style header, a paper line on every page, and 30 seconds of trace before each mark on sideways pages.
  • The diary asks "When?" after a mark: just now, 1, 2 or 5 minutes ago.
  • The viewer opens at a mark with 30 seconds before it and a step-back button.
  • Files handed to other people carry the Fairest Heart name.
  • Every small control has a 48 dp tap area; plainer words for TalkBack.
  • Under the symptom chips: when to call the emergency number.
0.993 – 6 October 2026Beta
  • The ECG tab fits the screen: trace, tiles, the recording bar at the bottom.
  • Recordings as one card per day, overnight nights on both days, one combined PDF per day.
  • Overview: a baseline card after every stop, the usual range as a band behind the heart-rate line, pull to refresh.
  • Strap battery chip turns amber under 20 % and red under 10 %, with the coin-cell type on the strap sheet.
  • "Connection lost" everywhere, never "link lost".
  • Renamed to Fairest Heart.
0.88 – 0.9630 September – 2 October 2026Beta
  • Time ranges: a day, a session or any range on the Overview, with its own Share.
  • Easy and Advanced interface modes.
  • Plans: Free, Plus, Pro and a Support purchase, through Google Play.
  • The first tab is ECG; HRV, accelerometer and strap panels are optional.
  • Record on connect, on by default, with a switch on the strap sheet.
  • Move a mark to the right second; multi-part diary labels; backdated marks.
0.78 – 0.8726 – 29 September 2026Beta
  • Dark look throughout, one type scale, big heart tiles on the Overview.
  • Five-step first run: a statement, permissions with reasons, strap fit, keeping the recording alive per phone brand, connect.
  • Every info sheet rewritten in plain language, the detail below it.
  • The clock may jump back (travel, daylight saving) without a recording ever being overwritten.
  • R-R tachogram in the EDF+ file, the PDF and the viewer.
0.60 – 0.77August – September 2026Private
  • A new Bluetooth link that survives a night without losing samples.
  • Insights: heart rate over the day, zones, Poincaré plot, signal quality, diary, last nights.
  • Recording files carry the strap's true sample rate in their header.
  • Hundreds of nights recorded by its first user before anyone else saw it.
