Changelog

What changed, release by release.

The version is on the menu's bottom line in the app. Google Play updates in the background. Earlier numbers are the test builds the app went through before anyone else saw it.

0.106.1326 October 2026Release
  • Sharing with an AI chat now sends exactly what you selected, a day, a recording or a marked moment, with no list of hours to choose. If it is too big for a chat app, the ECG trace is kept for the hours with your marks: one line says so, and "Change hours" lets you pick. The beats, rhythm and notes always cover everything you selected.
  • A marked moment's Share now asks who it is for, like every other Share: a clinician, an AI chat, or anyone else.
  • The Terms of use and the line about your recordings on the first-steps page are now links you can tap to read in full.
  • Insights: the space between the cards is the same everywhere, also under Recordings and on a recording's own page.
0.106.1226 October 2026Release
  • Sharing a whole day with an AI chat now starts with every hour of the day ticked, so every recording goes. If the whole day is too big to send at once, or the phone is short of space, the sheet says so and keeps the hours with your marks ticked.
  • A recording that was running when the app was updated or closed now carries on by itself the next time the app starts, also when "Reconnect on launch" is off. If it stopped more than 12 hours earlier, the app tells you instead.
  • The Terms of use are asked for once more after this update, and the tick box and "Accept and continue" now stay in view on small screens.
  • Marked events now also shows marks made while no ECG was recording: the time, your words, and "No ECG was recorded at this moment".
  • Sharing one recording now says when its PDF was left out, instead of sending without it.
  • iPhone: the app is made for iPhone only.
  • Clinical build: the Clinical tab now looks like Insights, with the same day bar and only the clinical cards; the summary table stays in the PDF.
0.106.1125 October 2026Release
  • Stronger terms of use: the first steps ask you to accept them, and Help links to them (fairestheart.com/terms.html).
  • Restarting a recording shortly after the app was updated or closed now continues the same wearing, instead of starting a new one.
  • A whole-wearing ECG file (EDF+) now marks every real stop with its length, also when the app was closed, and a second copy of the same time is named and left out instead of being added twice. The AI chat files describe the gaps the same way.
  • A copy in Downloads always carries its recording's own name.
0.106.1025 October 2026Release
  • The ECG viewer now carries on across a long recording's parts: scroll past the end of one 6-hour part and it continues into the next, with the clock and your marks following. "ECG in N parts" on the day card is gone.
  • The signal strip on the Recordings card is calmer: it is judged in 5-minute blocks, so single flickering minutes no longer turn it into a barcode. Poor signal always stays visible, however short.
  • Marked events: every row's time, words and heart rate now sit on the same line as its buttons, with room before the ECG button.
  • Sharing a set of recordings now leaves out, and names, a recording that cannot be read, instead of counting it as a failure.
  • On older Android phones (8 and 9) with both apps installed, a copy in Downloads is never written over a file of the same name from the other app.
0.106.925 October 2026Release
  • A long wearing now shows as one row on the day card instead of one row for every 6 hours, with one Insights, ECG and Share for the whole wearing. Delete asks once and removes all its parts.
  • Sharing a day with an AI chat now offers only that day's hours, under "Choose hours", with the hours that matter already ticked.
  • The first Share choice now says "A clinician" in every version of the app.
  • The mark button says "I feel something" everywhere, also on the recording notification.
  • Marked events: the ECG and Share buttons now line up at the right edge in every row.
  • Insights: resting heart rate, HRV and breathing are now one "At rest" card: three figures with a small bar for your usual range; the explanation sits behind the "i".
  • Insights: the Heart rate card shows three bars for the time at rest, active and asleep; its key moved into the "i".
  • Insights: the Recordings card has the same heading as the other cards, and its day strip now shows how good the signal was (good, fair, poor) with the time in each. The separate Signal card is gone.
  • Clinical build: new recordings are saved as FairestHeart_ files in Download/FairestHeart. Earlier recordings stay where they are, and the app and the Mac tools read both as one history.
0.106.825 October 2026Release
  • The Share sheet now asks who it is for: an AI chat, a clinician, or anyone else. Each choice has one line, and the file types are still one tap away.
  • Sharing to an AI chat is now its own page: tick the hours (hours with your marks are ticked first), choose whether your name and birth date go in, then tap the app to send to.
  • Sharing to an AI chat no longer includes a PDF: it sends the spreadsheets, the beats, your marks and a note that explains them.
  • The full recording (EDF+) is no longer offered to chat apps that cannot open it, on Android and iPhone.

  • Sharing a later recording of a longer wearing with a clinician or anyone else now includes the full recording (EDF+). Before, only the PDF and the spreadsheets went.
  • "I feel something" saves the moment at once, then asks what happened.
  • Easy mode: the ECG screen uses the same layout as Advanced, the recording bar matches Advanced's recording card, and the mark button says "I feel something".
  • On a small iPhone the live trace can shrink a little so the whole page fits.
  • First steps: tick to accept, plainer words for every age, and a plain battery line.
0.106.724 October 2026Release

  • The hour picker for AI chats now says the PDF's size next to the size of an hour, so the total under the hours adds up.
0.106.624 October 2026Release

Tapping it opens the mark. Each mark has its own row, and removing one mark removes only that mark.
  • "For AI chats" now asks which hours to send when a share covers more than one hour. Hours with your marks are ticked first; the size updates as you tick, and "Whole day" is still there.
  • Counting accuracy: a strap losing contact no longer shows up as a run of premature beats. Your recordings are measured once more in the background so their counts follow the new rule.
  • "Removing the sample…" no longer runs off the edge on a narrow phone.
0.106.524 October 2026Release

  • Sharing a whole night where one recording cannot be read now shares the rest and says which one was left out. That file gets its own name, so it never replaces a complete copy in your Download folder.
  • A recording with no ECG in it is reported as left out of a share, not as a failure.
0.106.424 October 2026Release
  • Sharing a whole night as one EDF+ file no longer fails because one of its recordings holds no ECG (for example a recording that was on hold the whole time); that recording is left out and the rest of the night is shared.
  • A moment you marked while a recording was on hold is now kept when you stop it, as it already was when you disconnect.
  • Stopping or disconnecting just as a recording starts now counts what it recorded, instead of treating it as empty.
  • When the app tidies away an empty recording, its copies of that recording's small side files in the Download folder go too; recordings and their EDF files there are never touched.
  • Sharing one recording right after you cancelled sharing a whole day now waits for that recording's measuring as it should.
0.106.324 October 2026Release
  • If Android closes the app while a recording is on hold, the recording now comes back on hold when the app restarts, instead of recording again by itself.
  • Disconnecting while a recording is on hold and still empty now closes it, so Record on connect starts a new recording the next time you connect. A moment you marked while it was on hold is kept.
  • Fixed a rare case where stopping a recording right after it started could leave its summary empty (length 0).
  • If your phone is too full to finish a recording's backup copy when you stop, the app now tells you. The recording itself is saved.
  • An empty recording left behind by the app being closed while on hold is now removed instead of showing up as a 0-second recording, and a recording left unfinished by an older version is finished when the app starts and copied to Download.
  • The size shown under "For AI chats" now includes the PDF, so it matches the size check. After you press Cancel on a share, no "Analysing" message appears any more.
  • Insights hour marks follow a change of time zone straight away.
0.106.224 October 2026Release
  • If the phone's storage fills up during a recording, the recording file is no longer left damaged: it carries on once space is free again, and when the recording is saved it is completed from the backup copy the app keeps beside it.
  • If Android closes the app to free memory during a recording and "Start recording again after a restart or update" is on, the app now starts recording again by itself.
  • The app uses less battery while the strap is connected but nothing is being recorded or a recording is on hold, and keeps the phone awake while the strap is out of reach so it can reconnect.
  • While the strap is out of reach, the notification now says the app is reconnecting to it, instead of saying the strap is connected.
  • The ECG viewer's clock now matches the rest of the app while a recording is running: by the end of a long recording it used to run about 40 seconds ahead.
  • With Standard scale on, opening the ECG at a moment or a mark now shows that moment on screen instead of just past the edge.
  • If the app cannot work out a recording's figures, the ECG viewer and the Insights page now say so instead of spinning for ever.
  • Insights opens faster when you come back to a recording, and sharing a whole day as EDF+ uses far less memory.
  • "For AI chats" now checks the size first: a pack bigger than about 1 GB, or one that would leave the phone short of space, is not made; the app asks you to share a day or a recording instead. Your name and date of birth stay out of its PDF whichever way you share it.
  • Pressing Cancel on a share now also stops the measuring it was waiting for, and a share asked for while another is running says so instead of doing nothing. Two quick taps on Share in the ECG viewer no longer start two packs.
  • After you stop a recording, a failure to save the backup copy no longer lets the app start recording again by itself later.
  • The recording's backup file writes about 60 % fewer bytes to the phone's storage on long recordings; nothing you see changes.
  • Recording figures (not in the Play build): a shape's coupling follows one rule on every page and in the PDF, the hour chart and the worst hour agree on a short last hour, and a new Clinical page shows the summary card beside the tiles and follows the Insights page when it refreshes.
0.106.124 October 2026Release

The line under the choice says how big the files are. Your name and date of birth are left out unless you switch them on.
  • Behind the scenes: finishing a recording's figures in the background keeps going after one recording fails, and the app looks up its own files in the Download folder far faster on phones with many downloads.
0.106.024 October 2026Release

0.105.823 October 2026Release
