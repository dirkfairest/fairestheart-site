Changelog

What changed, release by release.

The version is on the menu's bottom line in the app. Google Play updates in the background. Earlier numbers are the test builds the app went through before anyone else saw it.

0.100.239 October 2026Release
  • The hour that comes twice when the clocks go back (1 November) is unambiguous everywhere: times in that hour carry their zone ("01:30 EDT", then "01:30 EST"), the time pickers ask which of the two you mean, and strips named with an offset are placed by it.
  • Sharing a session or a time range gives one combined report, like a day does, with the year on its title; full-disclosure pages cover the first 24 hours and the summary covers all of it.
  • Sharing the EDF+ of a session no longer stops because one finished part has not been analysed: the app analyses it first, and anything it still cannot include is named in the file and in the message. A partial file never replaces a complete one. When the EDF+ cannot be given, the plain recording file is offered instead.
  • Marks: a note goes to the mark you confirmed, not the newest one; a change the phone could not save says so and is not reported as done; a mark can be slid back into the earlier part of the same recording, and a mark pressed while stopped can be moved onto the beats either side of the stop.
  • Beat shapes always show a drawing when one exists, from the recording itself or from the stored bank, and say in one line why not otherwise.
  • After switching straps, the app reconnects to the strap you picked last, and lets go of the previous one first.
0.100.229 October 2026Release
  • The ECG tab's tiles line up again: the title top left, the figure and its line underneath bottom left.
  • The Stop and Exit questions count the whole recording, not only the part written since the last six-hour file change, and say its length in plain words.
  • A recording started while the Recordings tab is open appears in the list at once.
  • Free space: a copy in the Download folder that the phone had renamed with "(1)" now counts as the recording's copy, so the recording is no longer held back for ever. A list of saved strips that cannot be read is left alone instead of being rewritten from nothing, and the phone tells you.
  • The strap list shows a strap as paired only when the phone says so, and a paired strap is preferred when the app connects by itself.
  • Opening the recording that is still being written never analyses it as a finished file.
  • Cards and pills show a ripple when tapped.
  • One rule for sleep stages everywhere: "awake in bed" and "out of bed" count as awake.
0.100.219 October 2026Release
  • Clinical: the dots on a run in the Overview's Rhythm card label the whole run again, so a false couplet can be dismissed from where you look, not only from the recording page.
0.100.209 October 2026Release
  • Share on a finished recording works again while the next one is being recorded: the recording still running is left out of the shared EDF+ file and the message says so. Sharing the running recording itself says plainly that it is still in progress.
0.100.199 October 2026Release
  • A recording that ended without closing properly (the phone closed the app, a reboot, an update) is now analysed the next time the app opens, so its day no longer says "not analysed yet" with empty cards. Before, only a recording that closed normally was analysed by itself.
  • On the Overview's top card, the way back from one recording to its whole day is a "Whole day" chip where the recordings chip sits, instead of a row under the bar.
0.100.188 October 2026Release
  • Shared PDFs carry the time they were made in their file details, the same time printed on their pages.
0.100.178 October 2026Release
  • A stopped recording is never silent: a notification tells you when the app was closed by the phone, when the recording stopped for any other reason, and when the strap has been out of reach for five minutes. Tap it to reconnect.
  • Delete also removes the recording's copy from the Download/FairestHeart folder, and the question says so.
  • Date of birth on three wheels; sex as three choices.
0.100.15October 2026First release

First release on Google Play. Records, shows and shares your Polar H10 ECG, heart rate, R-R intervals and HRV. Everything stays on your phone.

  • A sample recording to explore the viewer and the day view before you own a strap.
  • Copies of your recordings go to the phone's Download/FairestHeart folder, named FairestHeart_date_time.
  • The Help page links to the privacy policy, this website and the support address.
  • The live R-R, HRV and pNN50 tiles are on the ECG tab from the start.
  • PDF with a hospital-style header, a paper line on every page, and 30 seconds of trace before each mark on sideways pages.
  • The diary asks "When?" after a mark: just now, 1, 2 or 5 minutes ago.
  • The viewer opens at a mark with 30 seconds before it and a step-back button.
  • Files handed to other people carry the Fairest Heart name.
  • Every small control has a 48 dp tap area; plainer words for TalkBack.
  • Under the symptom chips: when to call the emergency number.
0.993 – 6 October 2026Beta
  • The ECG tab fits the screen: trace, tiles, the recording bar at the bottom.
  • Recordings as one card per day, overnight nights on both days, one combined PDF per day.
  • Overview: a baseline card after every stop, the usual range as a band behind the heart-rate line, pull to refresh.
  • Strap battery chip turns amber under 20 % and red under 10 %, with the coin-cell type on the strap sheet.
  • "Connection lost" everywhere, never "link lost".
  • Renamed to Fairest Heart.
0.88 – 0.9630 September – 2 October 2026Beta

  • Easy and Advanced interface modes.
  • Plans: Free, Plus, Pro and a Support purchase, through Google Play.
  • The first tab is ECG; HRV, accelerometer and strap panels are optional.
  • Record on connect, on by default, with a switch on the strap sheet.
  • Move a mark to the right second; multi-part diary labels; backdated marks.
0.78 – 0.8726 – 29 September 2026Beta
  • Dark look throughout, one type scale, big heart tiles on the Overview.
  • Five-step first run: a statement, permissions with reasons, strap fit, keeping the recording alive per phone brand, connect.
  • Every info sheet rewritten in plain language, the detail below it.
  • The clock may jump back (travel, daylight saving) without a recording ever being overwritten.
  • R-R tachogram in the EDF+ file, the PDF and the viewer.

  • Insights: heart rate over the day, zones, Poincaré plot, signal quality, diary, last nights.

  • Hundreds of nights recorded by its first user before anyone else saw it.
