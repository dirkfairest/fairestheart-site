Privacy policy

Everything stays on your phone. We receive nothing.

Effective date: 2026-10-26 · Applies to: the app "Fairest Heart" on Android and on iPhone, published by Fairest Media LLC. Contact: help@fairestheart.com

Short version: the app has no internet access. Everything it records stays on your phone until you choose to share it. We receive nothing from the app.

1. What the app does

Fairest Heart records, displays and exports the data your Polar H10 chest strap provides over Bluetooth: the single-lead ECG trace, heart rate, R-R intervals, heart-rate variability (HRV), strap contact and motion. It is not a medical device and does not analyse your heart rhythm.

2. Data the app stores on your phone

All of the following is stored only on your phone: in the app's private storage, and, for the recording files, also as a copy you can reach from other apps: on Android in the phone's public Download/FairestHeart folder, on iPhone in the Fairest Heart folder of the Files app (see section 3).

You can leave every profile field empty. The app works the same.

3. Data that leaves your phone

Nothing leaves your phone unless you send it. On Android the release build declares no internet permission, so Android does not let it open a network connection at all. On iPhone the app contains no code that talks to the network; it sends nothing.

  • Sharing. When you tap Share, your phone's share sheet opens and you choose the app that receives the file (your e-mail app, a cloud drive, a message). From that moment the receiving app's privacy policy applies, not ours.
  • One-tap backup (Pro). The same share sheet, for every recording not yet handed over. The app records that it handed the files over; it does not upload anything itself.
  • Copies other apps can open. The app automatically writes a copy of every finished recording (the EDF+ file, the heart-rate and R-R table, your marks and diary notes, and the dropout logs) to a folder you can reach from other apps: on Android the phone's public Download/FairestHeart folder, on iPhone the Fairest Heart folder in the Files app (On My iPhone › Fairest Heart). So other apps on your phone can open them, and on Android a recording survives if the app is removed. These copies stay on your phone; on Android any app you have given storage access can read them, on iPhone any app you open them with through Files. The EDF+ copy carries the profile fields from section 2 in its header.
  • Store purchases. Subscriptions and one-time Support purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store on your device. The store tells the app which plan is active. The app never sees your payment details. Google's or Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.
  • Tester code. If you open the tester-code page and ask for a code, the app shows a request code derived from a device identifier: on Android the Android ID, on iPhone the identifier Apple gives this app for your device (it changes if you remove all our apps). The code is only sent if you copy it into a message yourself. It lets us issue a code that works on your phone only; we keep the request code with your message and nothing else.

4. Data we collect

None. We run no server for this app, we have no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers and no accounts. If you e-mail us for support, we keep your e-mail and what you wrote to answer it.

5. Health Connect and Apple Health

This version of the app does not read or write Health Connect on Android or Apple Health on iPhone, and declares no Health Connect permissions. If a later version adds an optional sync to either, it will be off by default, ask for permission first, and this policy will be updated before that version is published.

On iPhone the app asks for two things only: Bluetooth, to find and talk to your Polar H10, also while the phone is locked (the app's Bluetooth background mode is what keeps an overnight recording going), and notifications, for the "recording stopped" notice and the heart-rate alert while the app is in the background. There is no location, battery or start-after-reboot permission on iPhone, and an iPhone cannot start a recording again after a restart or an app update: the app only tells you that the recording ended, and you start the next one yourself.

7. Deleting your data

  • Delete a recording in the app, or every recording from the Recordings tab. This removes the app's own copy. The copy in Download/FairestHeart (Android) or in the Files app (iPhone) stays until you delete it there (we are changing this so that Delete removes both; until then the app tells you).
  • Clear the profile fields in Settings. Recordings already written keep the header they were written with.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its private storage. On Android the copies in Download/FairestHeart remain yours to delete there; on iPhone uninstalling also removes the Fairest Heart folder in Files. Anything you shared elsewhere remains where you sent it.
  • The app does not back up to Google's cloud backup (Android Auto Backup is disabled for this app). On iPhone the app asks iOS to keep its working recordings out of iCloud and computer backups; the copies in the Files folder are ordinary files and follow your iPhone's own backup settings.

8. Children

The app is for adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18 and we knowingly collect no data from anyone, of any age.
