Privacy policy — Fairest Heart

Effective date: 2026-10-07 · Applies to: the Android app "Fairest Heart" published by Fairest Media LLC. Contact: help@fairestmedia.com

Short version: the app has no internet access. Everything it records stays on your phone until you choose to share it. We receive nothing from the app.

1. What the app does

Fairest Heart records, displays and exports the data your Polar H10 chest strap provides over Bluetooth: the single-lead ECG trace, heart rate, R-R intervals, heart-rate variability (HRV), strap contact and motion. It is not a medical device and does not analyse your heart rhythm.

2. Data the app stores on your phone

All of the following is stored only on your phone: in the app's private storage, and, for the recording files, also as a copy in the phone's public Download/PulseTrace folder (see section 3).

3. Data that leaves your phone

Nothing leaves your phone unless you send it. The release build of the app declares no internet permission; Android does not let it open a network connection at all.

  • Sharing. When you tap Share, Android's share sheet opens and you choose the app that receives the file (your e-mail app, a cloud drive, a message). From that moment the receiving app's privacy policy applies, not ours.
  • One-tap backup (Pro). The same share sheet, for every recording not yet handed over. The app records that it handed the files over; it does not upload anything itself.
  • Copies in Downloads. The app automatically writes a copy of every finished recording (the EDF+ file, the heart-rate and R-R table, your marks and diary notes, and the dropout logs) to the phone's public Download/PulseTrace folder, so other apps on your phone can open them and so a recording survives if the app is removed. These copies stay on your phone; any app you have given storage access can read them. The EDF+ copy carries the profile fields from section 2 in its header.
  • Google Play purchases. Subscriptions and one-time Support purchases are handled by Google Play on your device. Google Play tells the app which plan is active. The app never sees your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase.
  • Tester code. If you open the tester-code page and ask for a code, the app shows a request code derived from your phone's Android ID. The code is only sent if you copy it into a message yourself. It lets us issue a code that works on your phone only; we keep the request code with your message and nothing else.

4. Data we collect

None. We run no server for this app, we have no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers and no accounts. If you e-mail us for support, we keep your e-mail and what you wrote to answer it.

5. Health Connect

This version of the app does not use Health Connect and declares no Health Connect permissions. If a later version adds an optional sync to Health Connect, it will be off by default, ask for permission first, and this policy will be updated before that version is published.

7. Deleting your data

  • Delete a recording in the app, or every recording from the Recordings tab. This removes the app's own copy. The copy in Download/PulseTrace stays until you delete it there with a file manager (we are changing this so that Delete removes both; until then the app tells you).
  • Clear the profile fields in Settings. Recordings already written keep the header they were written with.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its private storage. The copies in Download/PulseTrace and anything you shared elsewhere remain yours to delete there.
  • The app does not back up to Google's cloud backup (Android Auto Backup is disabled for this app).

8. Children

The app is for adults. It is not directed at children under 16 and we knowingly collect no data from anyone, of any age.
