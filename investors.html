Investors

Every heart deserves its own record. We are building the company that makes that normal.

Fairest Heart turns a chest strap millions of people already own into an all-day, all-night heart recorder, and puts the record in the wearer’s hands in the formats clinics use. It shipped. People wear it every night. Clinicians who have seen it want their patients on it.

If that is a company you want to be part of, we would like to talk.

Talk to us

Founder: Dirk Fairest. Survivor of a cardiac arrest, defibrillator wearer, the app’s first and longest user. The story.

This page describes the company. It is not an offer to sell securities and not a solicitation of an offer to buy.

Fairest Heart is not a medical device and not a replacement for a recorder from your clinic. It records, displays and exports the data your sensor provides. It does not analyse your heart rhythm and makes no statement about it. If you are worried about your heart, see a clinician.
