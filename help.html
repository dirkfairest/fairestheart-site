What you need

StrapA Polar H10 chest strap, any generation that streams ECG. Wrist devices, optical sensors and other straps do not work with this app. Its heart rate and beat-to-beat intervals have been validated against a reference ECG in peer-reviewed studies (Sensors 2026; HRV at rest and during exercise, Sensors 2022). Questions

Will it tell me whether my heart is fine?

No. Fairest Heart records, displays and exports what the strap provides. It does not analyse your heart rhythm and makes no statement about it. If you are worried about your heart, see a clinician and bring the recording.

The reason is the law, not the technology. An app that says anything about your rhythm is a medical device. In the United States that means FDA clearance first: a formal study against expert-labelled recordings, a quality system, and a budget far beyond a one-person project. The analysis the maker built for his own heart exists, and it stays unpublished until it can go through that process. If you or your organisation would like to sponsor that path, write to us. We are open to it.

How does a recording keep running with the phone locked?

The recording runs as a foreground service with a visible notification. On first run the app asks Android to leave it out of battery saving. Some phone brands need one more setting; the onboarding shows the step for your brand.

Where are my recordings?

In the app's own storage, and as a copy in the phone's Download/FairestHeart folder, as EDF+ and CSV. The copies survive if you remove the app. Delete them with a file manager when you no longer want them.

What is EDF+ and who can open it?

EDF+ is the open file format ECG and sleep software reads. A clinician's software, free viewers such as EDFbrowser, and research tools all open it. So does the viewer on this site: it reads the file in your browser and uploads nothing. The app also shares CSV for spreadsheets and a PDF for people.

The strap will not connect
  1. Check the strap's battery. A weak cell connects and then drops. The app shows the level next to the strap icon.
  2. One phone at a time. The strap talks to one app on one phone. Close any other heart-rate app, including a watch app, on this phone and on others nearby.
  3. The phone may remember an old pairing. In the phone's Settings, open Bluetooth and forget "Polar H10". Switch Bluetooth off and on, then connect again.
  4. Wet the electrodes and wear the strap snug against the skin.
  5. If it still fails, write to us and say what the strap icon and the strap sheet show.
Why does the strap signal say Poor?

Usually a dry strap or a loose fit. Wet the electrodes, tighten the band one notch, and give it a minute. The signal quality card on the Insights tab shows how much of a day or night was readable.

Can I use it for training?

Yes. Heart rate, time in training zones and an effort load are there, with the usual formulas (maximum heart rate from your age, Karvonen zones). They are fit to you, not measured for you.

Can I set it up for a parent or partner?

Yes. Install it on their phone, connect the strap, and choose Easy in App mode (menu). They see one screen with a big "I felt something" button and nothing else; recording starts by itself when the strap connects. You read the recordings on their phone, or they share a PDF or the EDF+ file with you. Switch back to Advanced any time.

Does it sync with Health Connect, Google Fit or my watch?

Not in this version. A later version may add an optional Health Connect sync, off by default. Nothing syncs today.

How do I cancel a plan?

In Google Play: Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Fairest Heart. The plan runs to the end of the paid period. The app keeps every recording for another 30 days.

Something broke

Write to help@fairestheart.com and say what you expected and what happened, and what the strap icon and the strap sheet show. We answer by e-mail.
