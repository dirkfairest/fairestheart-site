Sponsors

Help keep it accessible and independent.

Some people like this idea because of their own heart story. If you do, and you want it to stay accessible and independent, you can sponsor it.

What the money does

Three things.

Keeps the free plan free

Every plan records the same signal. The free plan stays free.

Pays for the iOS release

An iPhone version is in the works. It takes work and fees.

Pays for the work on the app

Fixes, upkeep and new features. There is no server, no investor and no ads.

What a sponsor gets

A line and a letter. Nothing else.

  • A line on this page, with your name or anonymous.
  • A yearly letter on what it built.
  • No say over the app. A sponsor does not decide what it does or says.
