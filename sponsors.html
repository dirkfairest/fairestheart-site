Support the work

Keep it free. Make it better.

Some people want this to exist because of their own heart story, or someone else's. If you do, a one-time contribution of any amount pays for the work that keeps it in the stores and makes it better. There is no server, no investor and no ads.

What it takes

An app in two stores is never finished.

3,200changes to the code since July 2026
150releases since September
22of the last 30 days worked on it, many from morning to past midnight
2app stores, each with its own rules, reviews and yearly fees
  • Staying in the stores. Apple and Google change their rules every few months. Each change means work, or the app is pulled.
  • Keeping up with phones and straps. New Android and iOS versions, new strap firmware. Every one needs testing, nights included.
  • Fixing what breaks. A recording that stops at 3 a.m. gets found at 3 a.m. and fixed before the next night.
  • Building what people ask for. Clinicians, researchers and wearers write in. The good ideas get built.
