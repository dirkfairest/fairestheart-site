The record of your heart should not need anyone’s permission.

In June 2026 the maker’s defibrillator fired. His electrophysiologist prescribed a heart monitor. The insurer refused it. Nobody could say what had happened. So he built a recorder that asks no one: a chest strap, a phone, every beat saved, all night, in the file format the clinic reads. Fairest Heart is that recorder, for everyone.

Who this is for

Three reasons people support it.

For yourself

You have felt it: the racing, the thud, the pause. By the time a monitor is fitted, it is gone. You want the record in your own hands, the night it happens.

For someone you love

A parent, a partner, a child with a heart that does strange things and no answer yet. You want them to have a record to hand to their clinician, not a shrug.

For everyone

You are angry that a recording of your own heart is rationed by prescription, appointment and insurer. You want the free plan to stay free, so nobody is gate-kept out of their own record.

What your contribution does

Keeps it free, independent and alive.

Free stays free

The free plan records every beat, all day and all night, and shares the file. No contribution, no subscription, no gate. Your support is what pays for that.

Nobody owns the door

No insurer, hospital, device maker or investor decides who gets a record of their heart. No server, no account, no data to sell. It stays that way as long as people like you pay for it.

It keeps getting better

Two app stores to stay in, phones and straps that change, and the things wearers, clinicians and researchers ask for. Contributions pay for the person doing that work.

Contribute · any amount
