What it does

Record. Mark. Share.

Every day you wear the strap is on your phone. When you feel something, one tap marks it. The record goes to whoever you choose.

Record

Every beat, all day and all night, saved on your phone. No account, no upload, no internet.

Mark

One tap when you feel it, and a word for what it felt like. The moment is in the record.

Share

A PDF for your clinician, with strips at your marks. The raw file in EDF+, the format hospital software opens.

“My defibrillator fired. Insurance refused the monitor my electrophysiologist prescribed. So I built a recorder that asks no one.”
Why it exists

Fairest Heart was built by its first user after a cardiac event, and he wears it every night. It records, shows and shares. It does not interpret. The record is yours, in open formats, to hand to your clinician, an AI, or a family member.
