FAIREST HEART

Your Polar H10 ECG, all night. Heart rate, R-R, HRV, marks. Stays on your phone.

Fairest Heart turns a Polar H10 chest strap into an overnight recorder for your heart's electrical trace. It records, shows and exports what the strap provides. Nothing is interpreted for you: you see the trace, the numbers and the night, and you decide whom to share them with.

  • Live ECG, heart rate and HRV while the strap is on
  • Overnight and all-day recording with the phone locked
  • A mark button for the moment you feel something, a diary note later
  • Share as EDF+, CSV or a PDF with strips at your marks
  • No internet access. Everything stays on your phone.
Fairest Heart is not a medical device. It records, displays and exports the data your sensor provides and does not analyse your heart rhythm. If you are worried about your heart, see a clinician.
