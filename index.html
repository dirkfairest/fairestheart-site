What it does

Record. Mark. Share.

Every session you wear the strap is on your phone. One tap marks a moment. The record goes to whoever you choose.

Record

Short sessions or long ones, including overnight, saved on your phone. No account, no upload, no internet.

Mark

One tap, and a note in your own words. The moment is in the record.

Share

A PDF report of your session. The raw file in EDF+, the open format most ECG viewing software reads.
