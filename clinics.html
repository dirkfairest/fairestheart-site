Clinics

A record of the days before the visit, for the price of a strap.

For a practice that wants the person it sees to arrive with a record. The wearer records all day and all night. The practice reads the file. The app makes no statement about the heart.

What the practice gets

A file the practice can open.

All day and all night

The wearer records on a Polar H10 chest strap, one they own or one the practice lends.

EDF+ or PDF

The file arrives as EDF+, which opens in the viewer on this site or in any ECG software that reads EDF+. Or as a PDF with strips at the moments the wearer marked.

The practice reads it

The practice reads and interprets. The app records, displays and exports. It makes no statement about the heart.

$10 per wearer per month, or $300 per practice.

A loaner pool. Ten straps cost about $900. They wash. The app is set up once per phone.

How it works

Four steps.

The practice sets up

Choose per wearer or per practice. We send a batch of codes.

The wearer gets a strap and a code

Their own Polar H10, or one from the practice. They install the app and enter the code.

The wearer records

All day and all night. One tap on the phone marks a moment the wearer wants noted.

The file comes to the practice

The wearer shares it from the phone as EDF+ or PDF. The practice opens it and reads it.

Who is responsible for what

The practice reads and interprets. Fairest Heart records, displays and exports. Fairest Media LLC holds no data: nothing leaves the wearer's phone unless the wearer shares it.
