Accessibility

Fairest Heart should work for everyone.

This page says what we aim for, what we have done so far and where we still fall short.

What we aim for

We want this website and the app to be usable by people who work with a keyboard, a magnifier, larger text, speech output or a switch. Our target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

Where we are

In November 2026 we reviewed the website and the app against that target and began fixing what we found. The work is ongoing. We do not claim that the website or the app fully meets the guidelines today.

What we know falls short

The charts and reports are not yet fully described for people who use speech output: the live ECG trace, the trace in the viewer and the PDF reports are mostly visual, and we are still adding text alternatives. Some parts of the app and the site have also not yet been tried with assistive technology on real devices.

Tell us about a barrier

If something does not work for you, write to help@fairestheart.com. A person answers. Say which page or part of the app it was, what you were trying to do and what you use to read or navigate, and we will look at it. The contact page has the details.
